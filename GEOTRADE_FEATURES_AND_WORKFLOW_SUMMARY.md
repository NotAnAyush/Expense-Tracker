# GeoTrade v2.0: Platform Features & Trading Workflow Summary

> **A Plain-Language, User-Centric Guide to How GeoTrade Analyzes Geopolitics and Dispatches Market Signals**

---

## 1. What is GeoTrade?

**GeoTrade v2.0** is an intelligent geopolitical trading terminal. While traditional trading terminals (like Bloomberg or FactSet) display news headlines in text feeds, GeoTrade transforms global chaos into actionable market signals. 

It does this by:
1. Continuously scanning international news, military communications, satellite data, and government policy.
2. Calculating a live "fear score" (the **Global Tension Index**) for every country on Earth.
3. Forecasting how specific geopolitical shocks will move commodities (Gold, Crude Oil, Natural Gas), equities (S&P 500, Defense Stocks), currencies (EUR/USD, DXY), and digital assets (Bitcoin).
4. Generating actionable trade setups complete with Entry Prices, Stop Losses, Profit Targets, and Mathematical Position Sizes.

## 2. Core Platform Features

### 🌍 Feature 1: Earth Pulse (3D Interactive Globe)
*The central command visualizer.*
- **Interactive 3D Earth:** An interactive 3D globe with realistic dark-mode atmosphere, starry space background, and country borders rendered via WebGL.
- **Tension Heatmaps:** Countries change color dynamically based on their live GTI score:
  - 🔴 **Critical ($\ge 80$):** Active conflict, severe sanctions, major supply chain disruption.
  - 🟠 **High ($60 - 79$):** Heightened military posturing, political crises, trade embargo threats.
  - 🔵 **Medium ($35 - 59$):** Diplomatic friction, minor tariff disputes.
  - 🟢 **Low ($< 35$):** Stable trade environment, peace accords.
- **Geopolitical Conflict Arcs:** Glowing, animated 3D Bezier trajectories linking conflicting nations (e.g., Tehran $\leftrightarrow$ Tel Aviv, Beijing $\leftrightarrow$ Taipei, Moscow $\leftrightarrow$ Kyiv).
- **Hotspot Pulse Rings:** Concentric pulsating rings on specific cities or straits marking ongoing incidents with instant hover tooltips.

---

### 🗺️ Feature 2: Geo Map (Flat 2D Market Impact Matrix)
*For comprehensive geographical scanning and rapid asset drill-down.*
- **2D CartoDB Dark Projection:** Fast-loading vector map of all global sovereign territories.
- **Interactive Country Selection:** Clicking any country (e.g., Iran, Russia, Saudi Arabia, USA, Germany) opens a dedicated **Market Impact Drawer**.
- **Financial Correlation Terminal:**
  - Shows which assets are most sensitive to that country's actions.
  - Displays interactive **TradingView Candlestick Charts** with live price change percentages.
  - Breaks down the country's **Sector Exposure** (Energy, Defense, Agriculture, Tech, Metals).

## 3. End-to-End Workflow: From Event to Execution

```
[1. Global Event Occurs] 
   └── E.g., Pipeline shutdown or missile strike reported by Reuters / Twitter / GDELT.
          │
[2. Ingestion & Hashing] (<10ms)
   └── Sanitized, SHA-256 hashed, and deduplicated against Redis Bloom filters.
          │
[3. NLP & AI Classification] (~40ms)
   └── DistilRoBERTa assigns category ('energy_supply_disruption') & severity (0.92).
   └── spaCy extracts affected sovereign entities ('RU', 'EU', 'DE') & commodities.
          │
[4. GTI Score Recalculation]
   └── Global & Regional GTI meters update; 3D Earth turns amber/red in the region.
          │
[5. ML Ensemble Inference] (~15ms)
   └── Vol-Spike Model (LightGBM/XGBoost) calculates 81% probability of volatility surge.
   └── Directional Ridge Model outputs +0.74 Bullish Bias on Brent Oil and Gold.
          │
[6. Signal Generation & Kelly Sizing]
   └── Computes Stop Loss via ATR (Average True Range) and Target via 2:1 R:R ratio.
   └── Sizes position at 3.2% portfolio equity using Half-Kelly criterion.
          │
[7. Real-Time Broadcast]
   └── WebSocket dispatches payload to all connected browser clients.
   └── Earth globe pulses, sound/toast alert fires, and new Signal Card slides into view.
```
